## 🧠 INTERVIEWER EXPECTATION (CRUCIAL)

They look for:

*   ✅ Why pointers move the way they do
*   ✅ Why time complexity is O(n)
*   ✅ Correct duplicate handling
*   ✅ Edge cases (empty, size 1, all same)

> **If you can explain pointer movement clearly, you almost always pass.**
